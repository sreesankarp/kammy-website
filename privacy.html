Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 17, 2026

This policy is written to describe, plainly and accurately, what the Kammy app actually does with your data. It is provided as general information and is not a substitute for advice from a qualified lawyer, particularly for anything specific to your jurisdiction (such as GDPR, CCPA, or children's privacy law) before you rely on it commercially.

Kammy ("Kammy", "we", "us", or "our") provides a mobile application that turns spare phones and IP cameras into a peer-to-peer video monitor for babies, pets, and doorways (the "Service"). This Privacy Policy explains what information the Service collects, how it is used, and the choices you have.

By creating an account or otherwise using the Service, you agree to the collection and use of information as described in this policy.

1. The short version

  • Your video and audio never touch our servers. Streams travel directly between your own devices (or through an encrypted relay when a direct connection isn't possible), and we cannot see or store their contents.
  • Camera credentials for IP cameras never leave your device. Login details for RTSP/ONVIF cameras are stored only in your phone's secure, encrypted local storage.
  • We store the minimum account and device information needed to make the Service work: who you are, which devices and camera profiles you've set up, and whether you're subscribed.
  • We use a small number of well-known third-party services (listed in Section 4) to handle sign-in, push notifications, crash reporting, subscriptions, and — only for free accounts — advertising.

2. Information we collect

2.1 Account information

Kammy uses Firebase Authentication (Google Sign-In) to create your account. We receive your name, email address, and a unique account identifier from Google. We do not receive or store your Google password.

2.2 Device and camera information

When you register a device or set up a camera profile, we store:

  • A device identifier and the name you give the device (e.g. "Nursery phone")
  • A push-notification token, used to wake a camera or deliver alerts
  • Camera profile details: name, type (e.g. baby, pet, doorway), and — for IP cameras — non-secret connection details such as host, port, and stream path. Usernames and passwords for IP cameras are never sent to us; they are stored only on the phone acting as that camera's gateway, and shared directly between your own devices if a backup gateway is set up.
  • A coarse network fingerprint used only to judge whether a connection is on the same Wi-Fi network, for the purpose of applying the free plan's local-only limit

2.3 Alerts and usage

When a camera detects a loud noise, we log the time and an intensity level so the app can show you "quiet since…" information and send a push notification to your other devices. We do not record or store the audio that triggered the alert.

For accounts with remote (away-from-home) access, we record the number of bytes relayed through our infrastructure, purely to enforce a fair-use monthly limit. We do not — and cannot — inspect the content of that traffic; it remains end-to-end encrypted.

2.4 Subscription information

If you purchase "Kammy Anywhere", our subscription-management provider (RevenueCat) shares your subscription status and renewal dates with us so the app can unlock the correct features. Payment details (card numbers, etc.) are handled entirely by Google Play / Apple's own billing systems and RevenueCat — we never receive or store them.

2.5 Diagnostic information

If the app crashes or encounters an unexpected error, a diagnostic report (including device model, OS version, and the error itself) is sent to Firebase Crashlytics to help us fix it. These reports are not designed to, and do not intentionally, include your video, audio, or camera credentials.

2.6 Advertising (free plan only)

Free-plan accounts see ads served through Google AdMob, which may use an advertising identifier to serve and measure ads, subject to Google's own privacy policy and your device's ad-tracking settings. Kammy Anywhere subscribers do not see ads and are not served ads by AdMob.

2.7 What we do not collect

  • We do not request or collect precise location (GPS) data.
  • We do not store the video or audio content of your streams.
  • We do not store IP camera usernames or passwords on our servers.
  • We do not sell your personal information to anyone.

3. How we use information

We use the information above to:

  • Operate the core functions of the Service — pairing devices, establishing connections, delivering alerts, and waking a camera when a viewer wants to watch
  • Enforce the limits of the plan you're on (e.g. one camera on the free plan, remote access on Kammy Anywhere)
  • Diagnose and fix bugs and crashes
  • Communicate with you about your account or the Service
  • Comply with legal obligations

4. Third-party services we use

The Service relies on the following providers, each of which processes a subset of the information described above under their own privacy policies:

  • Google Firebase — authentication, push notifications (Cloud Messaging), and crash reporting (Crashlytics)
  • Cloudflare — hosts the signalling and account backend, and provides encrypted relay (TURN) service for connections that cannot be made directly
  • RevenueCat — manages subscription state for Kammy Anywhere
  • Google AdMob — serves advertising to free-plan accounts

5. Data retention

We retain account, device, and camera-profile information for as long as your account is active. Alert history and relay-usage records are retained only as long as needed for the purposes described above. You can delete your account and associated data at any time (see Section 7).

6. Data security

Video and audio streams are encrypted end-to-end using WebRTC's built-in encryption (DTLS/SRTP), whether they travel directly between your devices or through our relay. IP camera credentials are stored using your device's platform-provided secure storage (backed by the Android Keystore). Account data at rest is stored on Cloudflare's infrastructure with industry-standard access controls. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Your rights and choices

  • Access and correction: You can review and edit your device and camera profile information directly in the app.
  • Deletion: You may request deletion of your account and associated data by contacting us at the address below. We will delete your data except where retention is required by law.
  • Ad personalization: You can limit ad tracking through your device's operating system settings.
  • Depending on your location (for example, under GDPR or CCPA/CPRA), you may have additional rights, including the right to data portability, to object to certain processing, or to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority.

8. Children's privacy

Kammy is intended to be used by adults to monitor a room, a pet, or a doorway — it is not directed at, and is not intended to be used directly by, children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children through account registration. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will take appropriate action.

Because Kammy can be used as a baby monitor, footage may incidentally show a child; that video is never transmitted to or stored by our servers, for the same reasons described throughout this policy.

9. International data transfers

Kammy's infrastructure providers operate global networks, which means your information may be processed in countries other than your own. Where required, we rely on appropriate safeguards for such transfers.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. If we make material changes, we will notify you through the app or by other reasonable means before the change takes effect.
