Keep watch.Stay easy.

Any spare phone becomes a camera. Kammy streams it straight to your other devices, phone to phone, so you can check on a room without a subscription, a hub, or your video passing through anyone else's servers.

What Kammy does

📱

Any spare phone becomes a camera

No special hardware. Prop up an old phone, open Kammy, and it's watching.

🔒

Streams never touch our servers

Video and audio travel directly between your own devices, encrypted end to end. We never see or store it.

🎙️

Listen in, or talk back

Hear the room, and hold a button to talk through the camera's speaker when you need to.

🔔

Alerts when it matters

A loud noise wakes your other phone with a push notification — even if Kammy is closed.

📷

Your IP cameras, together

Kammy finds RTSP and ONVIF cameras already on your Wi-Fi and puts them in the same list as your phones.

🏠

Free on your own Wi-Fi, always

Watching from the same network you're on costs nothing and never expires — no trial, no catch.
